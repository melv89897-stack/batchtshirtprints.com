Quick Start Guide

Welcome to BATCH

Three tools to make print-ready t-shirt designs in bulk. No installing, no account — just open each file in your web browser (Chrome works great) and go.

Tool 1

Bulk Text Generator

Make dozens of text designs from one template.

  1. Open bulk-tshirt-design-studio.html.
  2. Type a template using a placeholder, e.g. {hobby} is my cardio.
  3. Add your list (one item per line) in the box below.
  4. Pick a font, color, and layout in the middle panel.
  5. Hit Generate — then download one design or the whole batch as a ZIP.
Tool 2

Pattern-Fill Studio

Fill your letters with camo, leopard, flag, and more.

  1. Open pattern-fill-text-studio.html.
  2. Pick a pattern from the library (or upload your own image).
  3. Type your slogans, one per line.
  4. Choose a heavy font, set the outline color and thickness, and arch the text if you like.
  5. Hit Generate and download.
Tool 3

Slogan + Graphic Remix

Pair one graphic with a whole list of slogans.

  1. Open slogan-graphic-studio.html.
  2. Upload your graphic (a transparent PNG works best).
  3. Paste your slogans, one per line. Add optional sub-text if you want.
  4. Pick a layout preset and text effect (arched, circle, etc.).
  5. Hit Generate and download.

Pro tips

  • Every file exports at 4500×5400, 300 DPI, transparent PNG — drop them straight into Printify, Printful, or Etsy.
  • Use the background toggle (Clear / White / Black) to check your design reads well on any shirt color before you download.
  • Bring your own slogans and artwork so your designs stay original — important for Etsy's rules.
  • Check trademarks on the free USPTO database (TESS) before listing — a phrase can be trademarked even if it seems generic.
  • Works offline once loaded — but the first open needs internet to grab the fonts.
